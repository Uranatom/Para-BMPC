# Original methods and third-party materials

- **CVaR-TMPC**: Y. Chen et al., “Interactive multi-modal motion planning with branch model predictive control,” IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, 2022, doi:10.1109/LRA.2022.3156648. Official reference: https://github.com/chenyx09/belief-planning, pinned commit in `configs/upstream_sources.json`.
- **Era-BMPC**: L. Zhang et al., “An efficient risk-aware branch MPC for automated driving that is robust to uncertain vehicle behaviors,” CDC 2024, doi:10.1109/CDC56724.2024.10886383. Official reference: https://github.com/Luyao787/Era-BMPC, pinned commit in `configs/upstream_sources.json`.
- **FOT-PID** follows Werling et al., “Optimal trajectory generation for dynamic street scenarios in a Frenet frame,” ICRA 2010, doi:10.1109/ROBOT.2010.5509799. Its polynomial and spline utilities are the unmodified pinned PythonRobotics files listed in `configs/pythonrobotics.lock.json`; the MIT license is retained in `third_party/PythonRobotics/LICENSE`.

The CVaR-TMPC and Era-BMPC code here consists of our common-model implementations/adaptations of the published optimizers. It does not vendor their official repositories and does not claim byte-identical reproduction of their complete original scenarios. Both use the declared two reactive hypotheses, shared vehicle/input model and prediction horizon. Their native optimization mechanisms and risk conventions are described in the manuscript. The optional official-source repositories are cited for inspection; no unverified upstream source redistribution is implied.

The remaining prediction/control/analysis code is author-developed. Dependency packages such as NumPy, SciPy, PyTorch, CasADi, ECOS, OSQP and Matplotlib retain their respective licenses and are installed separately. Weighted MPPI is the paper's feasibility-filtered weighted shooting variant without classical diffusion importance correction. LTV-QP uses two sequential linearized QPs with deterministic OSQP adaptive-rho interval 25. Fixed BMPC is limited to the stated finite search/confirmation procedure.
